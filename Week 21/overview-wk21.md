# **Week 21: Unit Tests & Test-Driven Development**

This week, we will explore the importance of unit testing and test-driven development, relating these practices to In Time Tec's focus on automated testing and the test automation pyramid.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the importance of unit testing in ensuring high-quality software
- Write unit tests for your project using a test framework
- Apply the principles of test-driven development (TDD) to implement a new feature
- Discuss the relationship between unit tests, TDD, and the test automation pyramid

## **Reading Assignment**

- Read Clean Code: Chapter 9, "Unit Tests" by Robert C. Martin.

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Write unit tests for your project, ensuring comprehensive coverage of the most critical components.
2. Implement a new feature in your project using test-driven development (TDD).
3. Document the unit tests you wrote and the TDD process you followed in a brief report.
4. Be prepared to share your experiences with unit testing and TDD during your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers.

## **Submission Guidelines**

- Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort with examples of the unit tests you wrote and a summary of the TDD process you followed. Open a PR with your name and week number, your instructor will look at the PR and add comments. Feel free to look at other students' PRs and generate a discussion. Example - dallen-pyrah-week-9

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share your experiences with writing unit tests for your project.
- Discuss the impact of unit testing on code quality and maintainability.
- Share the TDD process you followed while implementing a new feature and the challenges you faced.
- Discuss the relationship between unit tests, TDD, and the test automation pyramid.
