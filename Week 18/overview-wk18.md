# **Week 18: Interface Segregation Principle**

This week, we are focusing on the Interface Segregation Principle (ISP) and how it contributes to writing maintainable, extensible, and testable code, aligning with In Time Tec's emphasis on technical excellence. We will also explore the relationship between the ISP and the Quality Manifesto's focus on technical excellence.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the Interface Segregation Principle and its importance in creating maintainable, extensible, and testable code
- Apply the Interface Segregation Principle to refactor your project code
- Discuss the relationship between the Interface Segregation Principle and the Quality Manifesto's focus on technical excellence

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Identify areas of your project where the Interface Segregation Principle can be applied and refactor the code accordingly.
2. Discuss your changes with your mentor during your weekly meeting.
3. Be prepared to share your progress with your peers during your weekly meeting.

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share examples of the Interface Segregation Principle you applied to your project and the changes you made.
- Discuss the impact of your changes on code maintainability, extensibility, and testability.
- Share challenges you faced while implementing the Interface Segregation Principle and how you overcame them.
- Discuss the relationship between the Interface Segregation Principle and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on technical excellence.
