# **Week 14: Boundaries & Integrations**

This week, we will focus on defining and managing boundaries in code, connecting this concept to In Time Tec's emphasis on continuous integration and deployment. We will learn how to create clean interfaces between components, ensuring that our applications are modular, maintainable, and easy to integrate.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the importance of defining and managing boundaries in code
- Apply best practices for creating clean interfaces between components
- Identify boundaries in your project and refactor the code to improve integrations between components
- Discuss the relationship between clean boundaries and In Time Tec's emphasis on continuous integration and deployment

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Review your project and identify boundaries between components.
2. Refactor the code to improve integrations between components using best practices.
3. Document the changes you made and their impact on your project in a brief report.
4. Be prepared to share the changes you made and their impact on your project with your peers during your weekly meeting.

## **Submission Guidelines**

- Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort with an example of the changes you made to your project and their impact. Open a PR with your name and week number, your instructor will look at the PR and add comments. Feel free to look at other students' PRs and generate a discussion. Example - jdoe-week-8

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share examples of the boundary and integration improvements you made to your project.
- Discuss the impact of your changes on the modularity, maintainability, and ease of integration of your application.
- Share challenges you faced while refactoring boundaries and improving integrations and how you overcame them.
- Discuss the relationship between clean boundaries and In Time Tec's emphasis on continuous integration and deployment.
