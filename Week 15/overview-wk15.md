# **Week 15: Single Responsibility Principle**

This week, we are focusing on the Single Responsibility Principle (SRP) and how it contributes to writing maintainable, extensible, and testable code, aligning with In Time Tec's emphasis on technical excellence. We will also explore the relationship between the SRP and the Quality Manifesto's focus on technical excellence.

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Identify areas of your project where the Single Responsibility Principle can be applied and refactor the code accordingly.
2. Discuss your changes with your mentor during your weekly meeting.
3. Be prepared to share your progress with your peers during your weekly meeting.

## **Submission Guidelines**

- Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort with examples of the Single Responsibility Principle you applied to your project and the changes you made. Open a PR with your name and week number, your instructor will look at the PR and add comments. Feel free to look at other students' PRs and generate a discussion. Example - dallen-pyrah-week-5

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share examples of the Single Responsibility Principle you applied to your project and the changes you made.
- Discuss the impact of your changes on code maintainability, extensibility, and testability.
- Share challenges you faced while implementing the Single Responsibility Principle and how you overcame them.
- Discuss the relationship between the Single Responsibility Principle and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on technical excellence.
