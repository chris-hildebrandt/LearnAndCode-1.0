# **Week 9: Systems**

This week, we will focus on the principles of organizing systems, emphasizing how these concepts align with In Time Tec's commitment to modular, maintainable code and the Quality Manifesto's focus on technical excellence.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the principles systems and system organization for modularity and maintainability
- Apply best practices for organizing systems in your project
- Refactor your project's system architecture to improve modularity and maintainability
- Discuss the relationship between system organization and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on technical excellence

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share the changes you made to your project's system architecture, as well as the reasons for those changes.
- Discuss the impact of the changes on modularity and maintainability.
- Share challenges you faced while refactoring your project's system architecture and how you overcame them.
- Discuss the relationship between system organization and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on technical excellence.
