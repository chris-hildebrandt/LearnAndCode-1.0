This week, we will focus on Implementing and refining error handling techniques, and introduce error reporting/monitoring services thta you may encounter in your projects.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Apply best practices for handling exceptions and errors in your projects
- Enhance your project by implementing appropriate error handling
- Evaluate the effectiveness of your error handling strategies
- Understand the capabilities of error reporting/monitoring services like New Relic

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Review your project and identify areas where error handling can be improved.
2. Enhance your project by implementing appropriate error handling using best practices.
3. Document the changes you made and their impact on your project in a brief report.
4. Be prepared to share the changes you made and their impact on your project with your peers during your weekly meeting.

## **Submission Guidelines**

- Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort with an example of the changes you made to your project and their impact. Open a PR with your name and week number, your instructor will look at the PR and add comments. Feel free to look at other students' PRs and generate a discussion. Example - jdoe-week-7

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share examples of the error handling changes you made to your project.
- Discuss the impact of your changes on the robustness, reliability, and user experience of your application.
- Share challenges you faced while implementing error handling and how you overcame them.
- Introduce the capabilities of error reporting/monitoring services like New Relic and discuss how they can be used to improve your project.
