# **Week 1: Introduction to In Time Tec's Quality Manifesto & Clean Code Principles by Bob Martin**

Welcome to week 1 of the Clean Code course by In Time Tec. This week, we will be introducing you to the importance of clean code and how it aligns with In Time Tec's Quality Manifesto. Our focus will be on customer-centric design and how writing clean code can contribute to better solutions.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the importance of clean code and its relationship to customer-centric design
- Reflect on how clean code can contribute to better customer-centric solutions
- Choose a current or recent project to use as a case study throughout the course
- Share your reflections and the current state of your project with your peers and mentor

## **Reading Assignment**

- Read Chapter 1 of "Clean Code" by Robert C. Martin and review the main LearnAndCode README.

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Select a current or recent project that you will use as a case study for this course.
2. Further develop the discussion we started in our first session. Identify which areas of your codebase are well-structured and where improvements are needed. What aspects of your project do you wish to delve deeper into during this course?

## **Submission Guidelines**

- Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort. The file should contain your reflective thoughts, a brief description of the project you've chosen for this course, and answers to the questions mentioned above. Open a PR with your name and week number, your instructor will look at the PR and add comments. Feel free to look at other students' PRs and generate a discussion. Example - jdoe-week-1

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share your reflection on the importance of clean code and how it can contribute to better customer-centric solutions
- Introduce the project you have chosen to work on throughout the course and discuss its current state
- Discuss any challenges you anticipate in applying clean code principles to your project and how you plan to address them
