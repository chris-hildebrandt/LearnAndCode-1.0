# **Week 2: Meaningful Names**

This week, we are focusing on the importance of meaningful names in code, and how they contribute to clean code principles. We will explore best practices for naming variables, functions, classes, and other code elements to improve readability and maintainability.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the importance of meaningful names in code
- Apply best practices for naming variables, functions, classes, and other code elements
- Refactor your code to improve readability and maintainability through better naming
- Discuss the impact of meaningful names on code quality and maintainability

## **Reading Assignment**

- Read Chapter 2 of "Clean Code" by Robert C. Martin

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Review your codebase and identify areas where naming can be improved.
2. Refactor your code to apply best practices for meaningful names.
3. Document the changes you made and their impact on your code in a brief report.
4. Be prepared to share the changes you made and their impact on your code with your peers during your weekly meeting.

## **Submission Guidelines**

Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort with:
- Examples of the changes you made to improve naming in your code
- Explanations of your naming choices and their impact on readability and maintainability
- A summary of how meaningful names improved your code quality

Open a PR with your name and week number. Your instructor will review and comment on the PR. Feel free to review and discuss other students' PRs. Example filename: jane-smith-week-2.md

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss:

- Share examples of how meaningful names improved your code readability and maintainability.
- Discuss the most effective naming conventions you applied and explain your reasoning.
- Compare different naming conventions and their impact on code quality.
- Explore the challenges of naming in different programming languages and contexts.
- Brainstorm strategies for maintaining consistent naming conventions in your team's codebase.
