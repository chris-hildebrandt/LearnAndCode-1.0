# **Week 4: Functions**

This week, we are focusing on the importance of efficient and readable functions in code, and how they contribute to clean code principles. We will also discuss the relationship between these practices and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on clear technical documentation.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand how to write efficient and readable functions
- Apply best practices for function design that make your code more maintainable and readable
- Identify areas for improvement in your chosen project's functions
- Discuss the relationship between efficient and readable functions and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on clear technical documentation

## **Reading Assignment**

- Read Chapter 3 of "Clean Code" by Robert C. Martin

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Refactor your chosen project by improving function names and optimizing their efficiency. Identify areas where the function design could be clearer and more maintainable.
2. Document your changes and the reasons behind them in a brief report, highlighting the impact on code readability and maintainability.
3. Discuss your changes with your mentor during your weekly meeting, and be prepared to share your progress with your peers.

## **Submission Guidelines**

- Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort with an example of some changes you made and the reasons why you made them. Open a PR with your name and week number, your instructor will look at the PR and add comments. Feel free to look at other students PRs and generate a discussion. Example - dallen-pyrah-week-3

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share examples of efficient and readable functions you have implemented in your project.
- Discuss the impact of your changes on code readability and maintainability.
- Share challenges you faced while refactoring your project and how you overcame them.
- Discuss the relationship between efficient and readable functions and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on clear technical documentation.
