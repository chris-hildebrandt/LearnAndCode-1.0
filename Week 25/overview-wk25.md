# **Week 25: Concurrency & Performance**

This week, we will focus on the principles of concurrent programming and performance optimization, emphasizing how these concepts align with In Time Tec's commitment to delivering high-quality, scalable solutions.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the principles of concurrent programming and performance optimization
- Apply best practices for concurrency and performance optimization in your project
- Analyze your project for potential concurrency and performance improvements
- Implement changes to improve concurrency and performance in your project
- Discuss the relationship between concurrency, performance, and In Time Tec's commitment to delivering high-quality, scalable solutions

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Examine your project to identify areas where concurrency is currently used or could enhance performance.
2. Prepare examples of concurrency from your project, or propose areas where concurrency could improve the performance.
3. Discuss these examples or proposals with your mentor during your weekly meeting.

## **Submission Guidelines**

- Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort, including a description of the examples of concurrency from your project, or the areas where you believe concurrency could enhance performance. Explain the reasons for your proposals, if applicable.
- Open a PR with your name and week number, and your instructor will review the PR and provide comments. Feel free to engage with other students' PRs and generate a discussion. Example: jdoe-week-17.

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share the changes you made to your project's classes and system architecture, as well as the reasons for those changes.
- Discuss the impact of the changes on concurrency and performance.
- Share challenges you faced while analyzing and implementing concurrency and performance improvements and how you overcame them.
- Discuss the relationship between concurrency, performance, and In Time Tec's commitment to delivering high-quality, scalable solutions.
