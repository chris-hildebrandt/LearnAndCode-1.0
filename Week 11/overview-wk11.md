# **Week 11: Organizing Objects and Data Structures part 2**

This week, we will explore the principles of organizing objects and data structures, connecting these concepts to In Time Tec's focus on modular, maintainable code and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on technical excellence. We will learn best practices for designing and managing objects and data structures, ensuring that our software is adaptable, efficient, and easy to understand.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the importance of organizing objects and data structures in creating modular, maintainable code
- Apply best practices for designing and managing objects and data structures
- Refactor your project to improve object and data structure design
- Discuss the relationship between organizing objects and data structures and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on technical excellence

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Review your project and identify areas where the organization of objects and data structures can be improved.
2. Refactor your project by applying best practices for object and data structure design.
3. Document the changes you made and their impact on your project in a brief report.
4. Be prepared to share the changes you made and their impact on your project with your peers during your weekly meeting.

## **Submission Guidelines**

- Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort with an example of the changes you made to your project and their impact. Open a PR with your name and week number, your instructor will look at the PR and add comments. Feel free to look at other students' PRs and generate a discussion. Example - jdoe-week-6

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share examples of the changes you made to your project's object and data structure organization.
- Discuss the impact of your changes on code quality, maintainability, and efficiency.
- Share challenges you faced while implementing the changes and how you overcame them.
- Discuss the relationship between organizing objects and data structures and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on technical excellence.
