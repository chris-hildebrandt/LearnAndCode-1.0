# **Week 27: Documentation & Communication**

This week, we will focus on the importance of clear and concise documentation, both for internal use and client-facing communication, emphasizing how these concepts align with In Time Tec's emphasis on collaboration and the Quality Manifesto's focus on customer-centric design.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the importance of clear and concise documentation for both internal use and client-facing communication
- Recognize the connection between effective documentation, collaboration, and customer-centric design
- Review and improve the documentation of your project
- Create clear and concise client-facing documentation for a new feature
- Discuss the improvements and the new documentation with your mentor and peers

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Review the documentation of your project and improve it where needed.
2. Create clear and concise client-facing documentation for a new feature.
3. Review your progress with your mentor during your weekly meeting.

## **Submission Guidelines**

- Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort with a description of the improvements you made to your project's documentation and the new client-facing documentation you created. Open a PR with your name and week number, your instructor will look at the PR and add comments. Feel free to look at other students' PRs and generate a discussion. Example - jdoe-week-16

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share the improvements you made to your project's documentation and the new client-facing documentation you created.
- Discuss the importance of clear and concise documentation for both internal use and client-facing communication.
- Share challenges you faced while improving documentation and creating client-facing documentation, and how you overcame them.
- Discuss the connection between effective documentation, collaboration, and customer-centric design, and how it relates to In Time Tec's values.
