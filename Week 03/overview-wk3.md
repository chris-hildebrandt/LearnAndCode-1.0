This week, we are focusing on how developers can leverage AI tools and prompt engineering techniques to enhance their research and development processes. We will explore core concepts, best practices, and practical applications of AI in software development.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the basic principles of Large Language Models (LLMs) and their applications in software development
- Apply effective prompt engineering techniques to enhance developer productivity
- Identify and utilize AI-powered tools for code completion, debugging, and research
- Discuss the ethical considerations and best practices in AI-assisted development

## **Reading Assignment**

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Experiment with at least three AI-powered developer tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Perplexity, Cursor) in your development workflow.
2. Create a set of prompts for a specific development task (e.g., code refactoring, documentation generation, or bug fixing).
3. Document your experience with the AI tools and your prompt design process, including iterations and improvements.
4. Prepare a brief report on your findings, highlighting effective techniques, challenges encountered, and potential improvements to your development workflow.

## **Submission Guidelines**

Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort with:
- Examples of your prompts and how you used them in development tasks
- A comparison of the AI tools you experimented with
- Explanations of your prompt design choices and their effectiveness
- A summary of how AI tools impacted your development process

Open a PR with your name and week number. Your instructor will review and comment on the PR. Feel free to review and discuss other students' PRs. Example filename: jane-smith-week-2.md

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss:

- Share examples of how AI tools enhanced your development process and any unexpected challenges you faced.
- Discuss the most effective prompts you created for development tasks and explain your reasoning.
- Compare the performance and usefulness of different AI-powered developer tools you experimented with.
- Explore the ethical considerations and potential biases in AI-assisted development.
- Brainstorm innovative ways to integrate AI tools into your team's development workflow.
