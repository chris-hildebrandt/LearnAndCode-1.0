# **Week 8: Classes**

This week, we will focus on the principles of organizing classes, emphasizing how these concepts align with In Time Tec's commitment to modular, maintainable code and the Quality Manifesto's focus on technical excellence.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the principles of organizing classes and systems for modularity and maintainability
- Apply best practices for organizing classes in your project
- Refactor your project's classes to improve modularity and maintainability
- Discuss how we can create and refactor classes to align with the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on technical excellence

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Analyze your project's classes to identify areas for improvement in modularity and maintainability.
2. Refactor your project's classes based on the principles learned from the reading assignment and resources.
3. Document the changes you made to your project's classes in a brief report.
4. Be prepared to share the changes you made with your mentor during your weekly meeting and discuss the impact on modularity and maintainability.

## **Submission Guidelines**

- Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort with a description of the changes you made to your project's classes, as well as the reasons for those changes. Open a PR with your name and week number, your instructor will look at the PR and add comments. Feel free to look at other students' PRs and generate a discussion. Example - jdoe-week-08

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share the changes you made to your project's classes, as well as the reasons for those changes.
- Discuss the impact of the changes on modularity and maintainability.
- Share challenges you faced while refactoring your project's classes and how you overcame them.
- Discuss the relationship between class, system organization, and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on technical excellence.
