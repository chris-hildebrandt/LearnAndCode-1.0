# **Week 22: Design Patterns**

This week, we will explore various design patterns and their applications in software development, focusing on how these concepts align with In Time Tec's emphasis on technical excellence and the Quality Manifesto's focus on well-structured code.

## **Learning Objectives**

By the end of this week, you should be able to:

- Understand the purpose and benefits of design patterns in software development
- Identify and differentiate between various design patterns
- Apply appropriate design patterns to your project to improve code quality and maintainability
- Discuss the relationship between design patterns and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on well-structured code

## **Weekly Assignment**

1. Go through the resources above and choose at least 3 design patterns that peak your interest.
2. Write about your thoughts about those patterns and where they might be used. 
3. Document the design patterns you liked and disliked, the reasons for choosing them, and the thoughts your wrote down in a brief report.
4. Be prepared to share your experiences with the design patterns you choose in the meeting for this week.

## **Submission Guidelines**

- Submit a README file to the submissions folder for your cohort. Open a PR with your name and week number, your instructor will look at the PR and add comments. Feel free to look at other students' PRs and generate a discussion. Example - jdoe-week-10

## **Discussion Topics**

During your weekly meeting with your mentor and peers, be prepared to discuss the following topics:

- Share the design patterns you applied in your project and the reasons for choosing them.
- Discuss the impact of implementing design patterns on code quality and maintainability.
- Share challenges you faced while implementing design patterns and how you overcame them.
- Discuss the relationship between design patterns and the Quality Manifesto's emphasis on well-structured code.
